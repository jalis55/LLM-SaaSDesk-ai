FlowDesk Troubleshooting — Fictional Sample Data

Problem: Password reset email missing.
Steps: Check spam; confirm the account email; wait 60 seconds before requesting a new link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes. Contact support@flowdesk.example if repeated attempts fail.

Problem: Cannot invite a new member.
Steps: Confirm you are Owner or Admin. Check that the plan member limit is not reached. Ensure the invited email is valid. Invitations expire after 7 days; resend if expired.

Problem: Slack notifications are not arriving.
Steps: Confirm the workspace is Pro or Business. Ask Owner/Admin to check Settings > Integrations > Slack, reconnect if authorization expired, and verify the selected Slack channel permissions.

Problem: Projects are read-only after downgrade.
Explanation: The workspace exceeds Free plan limits. Reduce usage to 2 active projects and 3 members, or restore a paid plan. Excess projects remain viewable.

Problem: API returns HTTP 429.
Explanation: Workspace API rate limit exceeded. Honor Retry-After and reduce request frequency. Pro limit is 60/min; Business is 300/min.

Problem: Cannot export audit logs.
Explanation: Audit-log export is Business-only. Pro supports task CSV export, not audit-log export.

Problem: Payment failed.
Steps: Owner should verify payment method under Settings > Billing. FlowDesk retries on days 3 and 7. After 10 days unpaid, paid-only features become read-only.

Problem: Google Drive linked file inaccessible.
Explanation: FlowDesk only links to files. Check the original Google Drive sharing permissions; FlowDesk does not override them.
