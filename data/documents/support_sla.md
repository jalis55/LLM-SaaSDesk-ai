# Support Channels and Targets
All plans have access to help articles and email support at support@flowdesk.example. Free receives best-effort email support with a target initial response within 3 business days. Pro targets an initial response within 1 business day. Business receives priority support targeting an initial response within 4 business hours for severity-1 incidents and 1 business day for routine requests.

Business hours: Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC, excluding listed holidays. These are response targets, not guaranteed resolution times or contractual SLAs. Phone support and live chat are not offered in this fictional release.

Severity-1 means widespread service unavailability affecting most workspace members. Billing questions should go to billing@flowdesk.example.
