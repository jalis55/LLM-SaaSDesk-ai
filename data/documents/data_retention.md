# Data Retention and Export
Task and project data remain available while a workspace is active. After subscription cancellation, the workspace moves to Free and excess projects become read-only, rather than immediately deleted.

Workspace deletion has a 7-day recovery window. Following that window, data is removed from active systems. Backup copies may remain for up to 30 more days. This is distinct from canceling a paid plan.

Pro and Business can export tasks to CSV from Projects > Export. Business can export audit logs. PDF task export is not supported. To request a broader workspace data export, the Owner can contact privacy@flowdesk.example.
