# Cancellation and Renewal
Only the workspace Owner can cancel a paid subscription. Navigate to Settings > Billing > Manage Plan > Cancel Subscription, confirm the cancellation, and retain the confirmation email.

Cancellation stops future automatic renewals. Paid features remain available until the current paid period ends. The workspace then moves to Free, subject to Free limits. Cancellation does not automatically delete workspace data and does not automatically generate a refund. Refer to refund_policy.md for refund eligibility.

A canceled plan may be reactivated before the paid period ends from Settings > Billing > Manage Plan. Workspace deletion is a separate action described in account_management.md.
