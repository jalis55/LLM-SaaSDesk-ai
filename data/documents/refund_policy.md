# Refund Policy
## Monthly subscriptions
Monthly subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period begins. A duplicate charge or confirmed billing error can be refunded after verification if reported within 30 days of the charge.

## Annual subscriptions
A first-time annual subscription purchase is eligible for a full refund if requested within 14 calendar days of the initial charge. Annual renewals are not eligible for the 14-day first-purchase refund window. Duplicate charges and confirmed billing errors remain eligible for review within 30 days.

## Exclusions
No partial refunds for unused seats, early cancellation, or unused time after the 14-day first-purchase window. Taxes may be treated according to applicable law.

## Request process
Email billing@flowdesk.example with workspace ID, invoice ID, charge date, and reason. Never include full card numbers. Eligible refunds are issued to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days after approval. See cancellation_policy.md; cancellation alone does not request a refund.
