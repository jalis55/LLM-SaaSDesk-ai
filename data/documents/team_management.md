# Team Roles and Invitations
## Roles
Owner: full control of billing, workspace settings, membership, and deletion. Each workspace has exactly one Owner.
Admin: manages projects, invitations, and members but cannot change billing or delete the workspace.
Member: creates and edits tasks in projects they can access; cannot invite users or manage billing.
Guest: can view and comment only on explicitly shared projects; cannot create projects or export workspace data.

## Invitations
Owner and Admin can invite by email via Settings > Members > Invite. Invitations expire after 7 days. A pending invitation counts toward the plan member limit only after acceptance. Guests count toward member limits in this fictional product.

## Ownership transfer
Owner can transfer ownership at Settings > Members > Transfer Ownership to an existing Admin who has verified their email. Ownership transfer is immediate after confirmation.
