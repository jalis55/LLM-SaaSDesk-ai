# Accounts and Workspace Lifecycle
## Sign-in and reset
Users sign in using email and password. Password reset: Sign In > Forgot Password; a reset link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive, check spam and request a new link after 60 seconds.

## Email verification
New users must verify their email before joining a workspace. Verification links expire after 24 hours.

## Two-factor authentication
Authenticator-app TOTP is available on Pro and Business. SMS-based two-factor authentication is not supported.

## Workspace deletion
Only the Owner can request deletion at Settings > Workspace > Danger Zone > Delete Workspace. Deletion enters a 7-day recovery window; after that, workspace content is permanently removed from active systems. Backup copies may persist for up to 30 additional days before scheduled deletion. Billing cancellation should be completed separately before deletion.

## Account deletion
A user can request deletion of their individual account under Profile > Security > Delete Account, but must transfer ownership of any workspace they own first.
