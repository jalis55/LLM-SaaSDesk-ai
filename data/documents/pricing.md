# Plans and Pricing
FlowDesk offers Free, Pro, and Business plans. Prices exclude applicable taxes.

## Free
$0/month. Maximum 3 members, 2 active projects, 1 GB workspace storage. No API access, no Slack integration, and no audit-log export.

## Pro
$12 per member per month when billed monthly, or $10 per member per month when billed annually (annual charge of $120 per member). Up to 50 members, 100 active projects, 100 GB workspace storage. Slack integration, CSV exports, and API access included. Audit-log export not included.

## Business
$24 per member per month when billed monthly, or $20 per member per month when billed annually (annual charge of $240 per member). Up to 500 members, unlimited active projects subject to fair use, 1 TB workspace storage. Includes Pro features, SSO (SAML), audit-log export, and priority support.

## Trials and upgrades
New workspaces may start a 14-day Pro trial without a credit card. Trial expiration returns the workspace to Free limits unless a paid plan is selected. Plan upgrades take effect immediately; the remaining billing-period amount is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. See billing_policy.md for details.
