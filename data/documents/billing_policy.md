# Billing and Invoices
## Billing cycle
Paid plans renew automatically on the monthly or annual anniversary of purchase. Billing uses USD; taxes may be added at checkout. The workspace Owner can view invoices under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## Payment methods
Visa and Mastercard are accepted in this fictional product. Bank transfer, PayPal, and cryptocurrency are not supported.

## Seat changes
Adding paid seats during a billing period creates a prorated charge for the remainder of the period. Removing seats does not issue an immediate refund; the lower seat count is applied at renewal.

## Payment failures
After a failed renewal payment, FlowDesk retries on days 3 and 7. If still unpaid after 10 days, paid-only features become read-only; data is not immediately deleted. Payment recovery restores paid features. Contact billing@flowdesk.example for billing errors.

## Upgrade and downgrade
Upgrades are effective immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. If a downgrade exceeds Free limits, existing excess projects remain viewable but cannot be edited until usage is reduced or a paid plan is restored.
